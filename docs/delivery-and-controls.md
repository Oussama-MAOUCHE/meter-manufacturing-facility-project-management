# Delivery & Controls

## Delivery structure

My direct involvement began in August 2023 on behalf of the project owner.

The execution period was organized around four core contract streams:

- two contractor scopes;
- two engineering-consultant / BET scopes.

The wider stakeholder environment also included technical control, laboratories, internal management and technical functions, procurement and finance teams, and specialist suppliers or contractors.

## Project controls

I followed the project through a combination of:

- Microsoft Project schedules and revisions;
- physical-progress tracking;
- action follow-up;
- schedule-versus-actual review;
- recurring project-status presentations;
- monthly and executive-level reporting.

I collected inputs from the different parties, reconciled them into project reporting and regularly presented status, issues and actions to executive management.

## Documentation

I prepared or maintained a substantial part of the owner-side documentation used for coordination, management review and formal follow-up.

Depending on the subject, this included:

- progress and status presentations;
- meeting and negotiation minutes;
- technical specifications / tender packages (cahiers des charges);
- contract and amendment documents;
- work orders;
- requests and formal transmittals;
- change and additional-work documentation;
- financial trackers and final-account work;
- provisional-acceptance and closeout documents.

## Procurement and selection

Preparing and contributing to procurement documentation was a recurring responsibility.

Within TABEL, this included the engineering-consultant / BET consultation package and selected technical infrastructure packages. I also participated in contractor and consultant selection, technical offer evaluation and negotiation activities.

## Contract and change management

Contract administration covered:

- execution follow-up;
- amendments and closeout amendments;
- additional and complementary works;
- work orders;
- stop and resume controls;
- schedule and scope impacts;
- price discussions and negotiations;
- work-progress statements;
- final-account (DGD) and closeout documentation.

## Technical coordination

The project crossed several disciplines:

- civil works;
- steel structure;
- architecture;
- electrical systems;
- HVAC;
- fire protection;
- utilities and technical rooms;
- IT/network and surveillance packages.

Drawings were working references during follow-up. I reviewed them to understand interfaces and execution constraints and coordinated missing information, modified plans, study gaps and technical-control observations with the relevant stakeholders.

## Cost and payment follow-up

Financial follow-up connected contract status with execution status.

The reviewed records include:

- contract and amendment values;
- work-progress statements;
- invoices and payment status;
- forecasts;
- remaining commitments;
- final-account / DGD information.

I maintained the project cost, payment and contract-status view used for follow-up, while formal approvals followed the company's normal approval process.

## Technical control and outstanding items

I also followed technical-control observations and punch-list / reserve items, coordinating the required responses with the BET, contractor and technical-control body.

This work continued through provisional acceptance and closeout.

## Provisional acceptance and closeout

Both main contractor scopes reached provisional acceptance during my involvement.

Closeout work then covered:

- punch-list / reserve follow-up;
- final-account / DGD work;
- closeout amendments;
- service and execution documentation;
- final financial reconciliation.
