# Project Timeline

This timeline separates the project's earlier history from my direct involvement.

| Period | Project stage | My involvement |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 – mid-2023 | Site/terrain work, design development, permitting, geotechnical and technical-control preparation | Project-history context only; I had not yet joined the assignment |
| August 2023 | Transition into execution-focused project coordination | My direct involvement begins |
| September 2023 | Main civil/steel-structure execution scope mobilizes | Project-owner coordination, planning, reporting and stakeholder follow-up |
| October – November 2023 | Detailed execution monitoring, technical-control follow-up, steel-structure procurement and recurring reporting | Progress/schedule tracking and executive reporting become a regular part of my work |
| December 2023 – January 2024 | Technical-study coordination, price discussions, design issues and procurement preparation | Coordination, negotiation and preparation of technical/procurement documentation |
| March 2024 | Second contractor scope and second BET / engineering-consultant scope mobilize | Contract administration, work-order (ODS) follow-up and coordination across parallel scopes |
| April – June 2024 | Complementary works, price negotiation, modified plans, stop/resume controls and delay analysis | Change-control, technical issue and schedule-impact follow-up |
| July 2024 | First contractor scope reaches provisional acceptance | Acceptance and closeout follow-up |
| September 2024 | Second contractor scope reaches provisional acceptance | Acceptance, punch-list / reserve items and transition into closeout |
| Late 2024 | Broader project reporting and selected infrastructure/IT procurement evaluations | Technical evaluation, reporting and remaining project follow-up |
| January – April 2025 | DGD/final-account, invoices, financial reconciliation and closeout amendments | Financial/contract closeout work continues |
| May 2025 | End of my SAIEG role | Direct involvement ends |

The main construction scopes reached provisional acceptance during my involvement. Punch-list / reserve closeout and financial/contract closeout continued afterward within the same delivery period.
