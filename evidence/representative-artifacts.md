# Representative Project Evidence

The working archive is much larger than the material published in this repository. The examples below show the main evidence families behind the case study.

## Project reporting

The archive contains a long sequence of dated project presentations from September 2023 through 2024. They track physical progress, schedule consumption, technical-control actions, procurement topics, open issues, financial status and site photographs.

A presentation updated **08 September 2024** recorded:

- Civil works & steel structure: **100%**;
- Remaining works: **97.49%**;
- Global project progress: **99.12%**.

See the sanitized [progress snapshot](../assets/progress-snapshot.svg).

## Planning and progress control

Reviewed evidence includes Microsoft Project schedules, revised planning files, progress trackers and action-follow-up records. These were used with site and stakeholder inputs to compare planned and actual progress and prepare management updates.

## Contracts and change records

The delivery period involved **four core contract streams**: two contractor scopes and two engineering-consultant / BET scopes.

Representative records include:

- base contracts and amendments;
- work orders;
- stop/resume records;
- additional and complementary-work files;
- price proposals and negotiation minutes;
- closeout amendments;
- final-account / DGD records.

## Procurement and technical evaluation

The reviewed archive includes cahiers des charges, consultation packages, offer-evaluation records and negotiation material.

Within the TABEL project, this evidence supports preparation or substantial contribution to several procurement packages, including the engineering-consultant / BET package and selected technical infrastructure packages, as well as participation in contractor and consultant selection and technical evaluation.

## Technical coordination

Meeting minutes, plan revisions, technical-control observations and study-gap records document coordination across civil works, steel structure, architecture, electrical systems, HVAC, fire protection, utilities and other technical packages.

One representative case combined a steel-structure procurement delay with a freight-lift / foundation design interface. The summarized case is documented in [Representative Issue](../docs/representative-issue.md).

## Provisional acceptance and closeout

The archive includes provisional-acceptance records for both main contractor scopes, punch-list / reserve follow-up, final-account work, closeout amendments and service/execution documentation.

These records support the public conclusion that the main construction scopes reached provisional acceptance and moved into contract and financial closeout during the period covered by the case study.
